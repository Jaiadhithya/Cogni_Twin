1. Problem Space

Small businesses lack enterprise-grade data infrastructure.

Traditional SMB data is fragmented across POS systems, spreadsheets, and isolated CRM tools. This fragmentation prevents holistic operational forecasting.

  • Siloed Systems: Cannot correlate foot traffic with supplier delays.
  • Reactive vs Proactive: Decisions are made after financial anomalies occur.
  • Resource Constraints: SMBs cannot hire dedicated data science teams.

3. System Architecture

  • Ingestion Layer: FastAPI endpoints digesting asynchronous payloads (CSV, JSON, XML).
  • Simulation Core: Vectorized PyTorch tensors mapping state transitions of inventory and capital.
  • Insight Engine: LLM-based interpretation of anomalies (RAG architecture).
  • Client Dashboard: Real-time visualization utilizing WebGL and Chart.js.
Paper Research

4. Simulating SMB Data Elasticity

This presentation and the CogniTwin architecture are derived directly from our foundational paper research: "Simulating SMB Data Elasticity using LSTM-Markov Digital Twins".

Primary Research Objective

To prove that sparse, low-frequency data typical of small businesses can train highly accurate predictive state machines when augmented with synthetic Gaussian noise.

Key Finding: Overfitting Reduction

Our experiments demonstrated a 40% reduction in model overfitting when applying domain-specific data augmentation versus standard time-series forecasting.

Methodology

We hybridized an LSTM neural network with a Markov chain to simulate 'what-if' scenarios based on strict historical SMB constraints.

Academic Gap Addressed

Existing literature focuses heavily on enterprise digital twins (e.g., manufacturing). Our research successfully applies digital twin theory to micro-economic environments.
