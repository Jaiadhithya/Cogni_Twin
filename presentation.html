The Challenge

Real-world sales data is inherently noisy, incomplete, and highly dimensional. Predictive accuracy often comes at the cost of transparency.

  • Data Quality: Missing values, inconsistent schemas, and human errors in raw logs.
  • Complex Features: Impact of weather, competitor discounts, and marketing spend.
  • The Black Box: Stakeholders reject models they cannot understand.
